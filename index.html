Frequently Asked Questions

The questions Microsoft Partners and enterprise buyers ask most often.

How long does a Microsoft 365 Copilot adoption rollout take?
Structured Copilot deployments typically break even in 3.5 to 4 months. The largest recent rollout, a 30,000 seat European enterprise deployment, reached documented ROI in 39 days using role-based enablement and the ADKAR change management framework.
What is the ADKAR framework and why does it matter for Copilot adoption?
ADKAR is a Prosci change management model covering Awareness, Desire, Knowledge, Ability and Reinforcement. Applied to Microsoft 365 Copilot rollouts, ADKAR moves organisations from around 20 percent to over 90 percent active user adoption within six months.
Do you work directly with enterprises or only through Microsoft Partners?
The primary sales model is indirect, working through MSPs, CSPs and Microsoft Partners who serve enterprise clients of 500 plus employees. Direct enterprise engagements are accepted on a case-by-case basis.
What size organisations do you serve for Copilot rollouts?
Enterprise scale, typically 500 to 30,000 plus seats. The most recent flagship project is a 30,000 licence enterprise deployment across Europe with 98 percent active weekly usage and documented ROI in 39 days.
Which languages do you deliver Copilot training in?
English, Italian, Greek and Polish. Multilingual delivery across European markets is a core differentiator, especially for Microsoft Partners serving multi-country enterprise customers.
What ROI can be expected from a Copilot deployment?
Audited results from the 30,000 plus seat rollout include 98 percent active weekly usage, 8 hours saved per user per month, 224,000 plus Copilot actions and 99 percent licence renewal acceptance.
