How these numbers are calculated

  • Assisted Work Hours / Year = licensed employees x share of routine digital tasks x industry AI-assistability factor x expected adoption rate x annual working hours. Driven by your answers, not a generic benchmark.
  • Economic Value / Year = Assisted Work Hours x your average fully loaded hourly cost. A CFO can read this as annual capacity value unlocked.
  • Change Manager Hours = recommended effort scaled by organization size, your readiness gap, and target rollout speed.
  • Forrester TEI studies (up to 353% ROI for SMBs, ~9 hrs saved/user/month) are used only as an external sanity check, not as the calculation basis.
